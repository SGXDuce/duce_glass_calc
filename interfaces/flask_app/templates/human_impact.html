Human Impact Check — Scope

This tool assesses AS 1288 Section 5 (Human Impact) Grade A safety glass requirements for a limited set of configurations. Please read what is and isn't covered before continuing.

Covered
  • Doors, side panels, and windows
  • Glazed as a fixed panel/sash, a louvre, or sashless
  • Residential, school/early childhood, aged care, and bathroom locations
Not covered
  • Shopfronts, stairways, balustrades
  • Mirrors or backed glass, wardrobe doors, roller doors
  • Shower screen assemblies, window seat glazing, leadlights, faceted glazing
  • Grade B glass anywhere
  • The vanity/bench exemption (Clause 5.8) and permanent-barrier exemption (Clause 5.24) — flagged where relevant, not assessed
  • Whether an opening is in an operable position, and oversize louvre blades (outside Clause 5.12's tabulated sizes)

This tool does not replace professional judgement under AS 1288. Always confirm the result against the current standard before relying on it.
